❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your photography style? +
We specialize in candid, natural-light photography that tells the real story of your day — blending documentary-style with artistic portraiture.
How far in advance should we book? +
We recommend booking 4–6 months in advance, especially for wedding season (November–March). Reach out early to secure your date.
Do you travel outside Bangalore? +
Absolutely! We cover weddings and pre-wedding shoots across India — from Udaipur to Goa, and even international destinations.
How long does it take to receive the final photos? +
We deliver a sneak peek within 48 hours, and the full gallery (300+ edited images) within 4–6 weeks.
Do you offer albums or prints? +
Yes, we provide premium handcrafted wedding albums and fine art prints as add-ons to your package.
