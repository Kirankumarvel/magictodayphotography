Why Families Book Us

Your Jubilee Deserves
More Than a Selfie

A jubilee photographer has one job: to be present without being in the way. That means knowing when the vow renewal begins, where the light falls in a banquet hall, and how to photograph a couple who have been married for fifty years without making it feel staged. It is less about gear and more about reading the room.

Since 2019 we have covered more than 200 jubilee celebrations across Bangalore. Silver jubilees at homes in Jayanagar. Golden jubilees in banquet halls in Rajajinagar. Vow renewals in Koramangala. Surprise parties in Whitefield. Every family has its own way of celebrating, and we have learned to follow without directing.
