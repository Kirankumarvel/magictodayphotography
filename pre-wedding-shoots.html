Why Couples Book Us

Your Story Starts
Before the Wedding

A pre-wedding shoot is a chance to be yourselves without the crowd, the rituals or the timeline of a wedding day. We photograph you in the places you already love, at the hours when Bangalore looks its best, and we keep the direction light. No stiff posing. No thirty-minute instructions. You talk, you walk, you laugh, and we shoot.

Since 2019 we have shot more than 150 pre-wedding sessions and 80 weddings across Bangalore and Karnataka, from Cubbon Park on a Sunday morning to Nandi Hills at sunrise. That experience shows up in small ways, like knowing where the crowds thin out and when the light turns gold.
