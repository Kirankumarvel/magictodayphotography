Our Story

Why Couples Across Bangalore
Trust Magic Today

Sathya started Magic Today after shooting his cousin's wedding in 2019. He noticed something: the photos everyone loved were not the posed ones. They were the ones where the bride was laughing at her own nervousness, or the groom's mother was wiping her eyes during the vidai. Five years and 150 plus events later, that is still what he looks for.

About Sathya: With a background in visual storytelling and a calm presence on shoot days, Sathya has been featured in wedding blogs for his ability to catch raw emotion. His approach is simple: document, do not direct.

Our candid wedding photography focuses on unscripted moments, genuine emotions, and true-to-life colours. We specialise in:

  • Candid wedding photography for raw, authentic moments
  • Pre-wedding photoshoots at Bangalore locations (starting at Rs 25,000 plus)
  • Newborn photography with safe, home-based sessions
  • Maternity photoshoots celebrating motherhood
  • Event photography in Bangalore for corporate and private functions
  • Professional portfolio photography for models and actors
