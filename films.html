How we work

Filmed with Feeling, Edited with Patience

Wedding films should not feel like a highlight reel on fast-forward. We shoot for the in-between moments. The nervous laugh before the muhurtham. The father adjusting the saree pallu. The friends who cry in the third row.

Candid direction

No stiff poses. We guide gently when needed, then step back and let the day happen.

Hand-edited films

Every film is colour-graded and edited in-house. No automated edits, no copy-paste templates.

Live sound and vows

We record the vows, the laughter and the music, so the film sounds as real as it feels.

Bangalore and beyond

Based in Bangalore, filming across Karnataka, Goa, Kerala, Udaipur and destination weddings.
