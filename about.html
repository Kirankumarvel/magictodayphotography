Meet the Founder:
The Heart Behind the Lens

Magic Today Photography started with Sathya's idea to move past posed setups and studio lighting. After watching too many couples settle for stiff, formulaic portraits, he set out to build something different: unscripted moments shot with cinematic care.

With a deep respect for the emotional moments that shape a wedding, Sathya put together a team that looks for the real laughter, the stolen glances and the joy that make your day yours.

"Every frame tells the story that only you can live."

What began as a passion project is now Bangalore's trusted name for candid wedding photography. Today Sathya leads photographers and post-production artists who share one focus: galleries that read like visual poetry.

Our Style and Philosophy

Beyond the Shutter

We do not force smiles or wait for a perfect pose. We look for the authenticity that unfolds on its own. The tears during vows. The laughter during the reception. The quiet exchanges that happen between the official moments.

Cinematic Storytelling

Every wedding is treated as a visual narrative, with attention to composition, natural light and emotional depth. Your day is not a series of isolated photos. It is a story that runs from the first look to the last dance.

True-to-Life Colours

The colours in your photos reflect the mood and atmosphere of the day, not an oversaturated version of it. Hand editing in our own colour style, never a bulk filter.

Cultural Familiarity

Years spent learning the details of Indian wedding rituals, from Muhurtham to Sangeet, Haldi to Mehendi. Your traditions are photographed with the respect they deserve.

Beyond Weddings: A Full Range of Services

Candid wedding photography is the specialty, but the studio also covers romantic pre-wedding shoots at Cubbon Park and Lalbagh, safe in-home newborn sessions and maternity portraits. Every service gets the same care.

Why We Are Trusted

Reliability, Redundancy, Preparation

Professional Equipment

Full-frame Sony and Canon bodies with dual card slots for instant data redundancy. Every frame is written to two cards at once.

Identical Backup Gear

Two camera bodies, multiple lenses and additional flashes travel to every shoot. Technical failure does not get a vote.

Experience Under Pressure

Across 150+ weddings we have handled power failures, weather changes and last-minute venue shifts. We stay calm and keep shooting.
