✦ Heart Behind the Lens

Meet the Founder: The Heart Behind the Lens

Magic Today Photography was born from Sathya's vision to move beyond the constraints of traditional poses and studio lighting. After witnessing too many couples settle for stiff, formulaic wedding portraits, Sathya set out to create something different: unscripted moments with cinematic excellence.

With a deep respect for the emotional moments that define a wedding, Sathya built a team dedicated to capturing the authentic laughter, stolen glances, and genuine joy that make your day uniquely yours.

"Every frame tells the story that only you can live."

What started as a passion project has evolved into Bangalore's trusted name for candid wedding photography. Today, Sathya leads a team of seasoned photographers and post-processing artists who share a single obsession: crafting wedding galleries that feel like visual poetry.

✦ Our Style & Philosophy

Beyond the Shutter

At Magic Today Photography, we don't believe in forcing smiles or waiting for the "perfect pose." Instead, we specialize in capturing the authenticity that unfolds naturally—the tears during vows, the laughter during receptions, the intimate exchanges that happen in between the "official" moments.

Cinematic Storytelling

We approach each wedding as a visual narrative, with careful attention to composition, natural lighting, and emotional depth. Your wedding day isn't a series of isolated photos—it's a cinematic journey.

True-to-Life Colors

Our post-processing workflow ensures that the colors in your photos reflect the genuine mood and atmosphere of your day, not an oversaturated or artificially filtered version.

Cultural Competence

We have spent years mastering the intricate details of Indian wedding rituals—from Muhurtham to Sangeet, Haldi to Mehendi. We celebrate your traditions with reverence.

Beyond Weddings: A Full Range of Services

While candid wedding photography is our specialty, our expertise extends to romantic pre-wedding shoots at Cubbon Park or Lalbagh, safe home-based newborn photography, and elegant maternity portraits. Every service is delivered with the same commitment to excellence and authenticity.
