✦ ETHICAL PHOTOGRAPHY & PERMITS

Love deserves a beautiful frame – but always with respect for public spaces

Behind every great love story is a setting that makes it unforgettable. Bangalore offers misty lakes, royal granite architecture, quirky graffiti lanes, and hidden quarries. But here’s the truth: many locations require prior permission for professional shoots. For Vidhana Soudha, early morning is the best time because at that time you can’t cause any problem for the public. Below, we’ve packed each spot with fun facts, real stats, pro tips, and responsibility checklists – so you can shoot confidently and ethically.

📌 In a Nutshell

Plan your Bangalore pre-wedding shoot with these 10 amazing spots. Always get permits, respect public spaces, and shoot early morning or golden hour for the best light. This guide gives you location stats, permission rules, and pro tips to make your shoot memorable and responsible.

1. 🎨 Church Street – Graffiti Walls & Candid Urban Vibe

🎨 50+ murals (rotating)
📚 Blossom Book House: 2.5L books
☕ 20+ cafes

Best time: 7–9 AM (soft light, empty streets). Stat: Weekend footfall ~10,000. Permission: Inform cafes for interior shots; public sidewalks free but avoid blocking. Responsibility: No loud music, keep tripod legs away from walkways.

📸 Hack: Use the pastel-coloured wall near The Bookshop for dreamy natural reflectors.

2. 🌊 Hesaraghatta Lake – Serene Waters & Migratory Birds

🕒 Best: 6:30–8:30 AM
🐦 200+ bird species
📏 450 acres
⚠️ Permission required

Built in 1894, this reservoir attracts over 30,000 migratory birds (Nov–Feb). Rocky shores give perfect reflections. Permission: Prior nod from forest/tourism department. Responsibility: No drones, keep 50m from nesting areas, carry back all trash.

🔭 Hack: Bring a 70-200mm lens for bird silhouettes; polarising filter cuts water glare.

3. 🏛️ Vidhana Soudha – Night Floodlit Sundays & Early Morning Best

🏛️ 4,500 tons granite
⏰ Early morning: zero disturbance
📸 Sunday floodlights 6–8:30PM

Early morning (6–7 AM) is safest – you won’t cause any problem for the public or government work. Floodlit Sundays offer dramatic night shots (tripod mandatory). Permission: Bangalore City Police approval required for professional equipment.

💡 Hack: Shoot from the public terrace opposite Vidhana Soudha for a majestic elevated angle.

4. ⛰️ Quarry Sunset Point – Muddayyanapalya

🌄 Golden hour 5:30–6:30 PM
🗻 Altitude ~900m
⚠️ No safety barriers

Hidden gem with white rocks and emerald water. Stat: Sunset colours last ~20min. Permission: Inform local panchayat before any commercial shoot. Responsibility: Stay 10ft from quarry edges, absolutely no glass bottles, carry all trash.

🧗‍♀️ Hack: Arrive 45min early to scout safe, non‑crumbling rock platforms for silhouettes.

5. 🦩 Madiwala Lake – BTM Layout (114 acres)

🐦 150+ species
🚶 2.5 km walking track
🎟️ Entry ₹10

Restored in 2016, hosts 300+ resident egrets. Misty mornings = ethereal frames. Permission: Lake Development Authority approval needed. Responsibility: No loud music, stay on designated paths, don’t feed birds.

🌅 Hack: Use the wooden viewing deck near the boathouse for unobstructed water reflections.

6. 🎋 ZEN Park, Nagarbhavi – Caution: public discipline & responsibility

☸️ 20ft Buddha statue
🌿 Meditation zone
⚠️ Mandatory permission

Japanese‑themed garden. Official rule: Professional pre‑wedding shoots are NOT allowed without written BBMP permission. If granted, extreme caution about public discipline and responsibility – no loud music, no blocking pathways, group ≤4 people.

🧘 Hack: Shoot only on weekdays when fewer visitors; use natural ambient sound, zero artificial music.

7. 🌳 Jayanagar Parks – Near Metro Lines (B.N. Vijayakumar Park & Mini Forest)

🚇 100m from metro
🌳 1,200 trees in mini forest
🐦 40 bird species

Best time: 6:30–8:30 AM. Permission: BBMP permit required. Responsibility: Stay on walking tracks, no climbing trees, respect morning walkers.

🚶 Hack: Use the arched bamboo tunnel near the mini forest entrance for frame‑within‑frame composition.

8. 🦋 Bannerghatta Hill – Scenic Wilderness & Butterfly Park

🦋 90 butterfly species
📈 Elevation 650m
🌲 National park adjacent

India’s first butterfly park (opened 2006). Permission: Forest department fees ₹500–2000. Responsibility: No flash photography near animals, stay on trails, no littering.

🦎 Hack: Macro lens + early morning = dew-covered butterflies on flowering plants.

9. 📸 MG Road – Urban Edge & Night Vibe

🏙️ 2 km heritage stretch
💡 200+ LED streetlights
🚶 50k+ daily footfall

Shoot early morning (7–9 AM) or after 7 PM. Permission: Tripods on public footpaths may need police nod. Responsibility: Don’t block shop entrances, be mindful of traffic.

🌃 Hack: Use the glass facade of the Methodist Church as a giant reflector for night portraits.

10. 🏡 A Scenic Spot Near You – With proper permission & responsibility

🏠 Zero travel stress
📸 100% unique to your story
🤝 Neighbour consent

Your own terrace, society garden, or tree‑lined avenue. Permission: Written NOC from RWA or local municipality. Responsibility: Shoot between 10 AM – 5 PM on weekdays, inform neighbours, keep music off, clean up after.

🏡 Hack: Use bedsheets as diffusers on sunny days; fairy lights + evening blue hour = magic.

💡 Golden Hour & Lighting Hacks You’ll Wish You Knew Sooner

The golden hour (first hour after sunrise, last hour before sunset) gives soft, warm, directional light that sculpts faces beautifully. Overcast days act as a giant softbox – perfect for even skin tones. Pro hack: Use a silver reflector to bounce light into shadows when shooting against strong backlight. Avoid harsh midday sun (10 AM – 3 PM) – if unavoidable, find open shade under trees.

📌 Frequently Asked Questions – Permits, Ethics & Practical Tips

❓ How long is a typical pre-wedding shoot and how many photos do we get?
Our standard pre-wedding package includes 2–3 hours of coverage at one or two nearby locations. You receive 50+ professionally edited, high-resolution images delivered in a private online gallery within 2 weeks.
❓ What happens if it rains on the day of our pre-wedding shoot?
Bangalore weather can be unpredictable. We keep a flexible reschedule policy – if rain is forecast, we can shift to a nearby indoor venue (heritage bungalow, studio, or covered courtyard) at no extra cost, or postpone to another mutually convenient date. Your deposit remains fully adjustable.
❓ What permissions are needed for Vidhana Soudha?
Mandatory permission from Bangalore City Police. Early morning (6-7 AM) is best because you won't cause any public disturbance. No tripods or professional equipment without approval.
❓ Is Zen Park allowed for pre-wedding shoots?
Not without written permission from BBMP. Even if granted, extreme caution about public discipline and responsibility – no loud music, no blocking pathways, group ≤4. This is a meditation zone.
❓ Best time for Hesaraghatta Lake?
6:30–8:30 AM during Nov–Feb for migratory birds. Prior permission from forest department may be needed. Weekdays are less crowded.
❓ Do we need permission for Madiwala Lake?
Yes, from Lake Development Authority. Professional shoots with reflectors/tripods require prior approval. Entry ₹10.
❓ What is the best month for outdoor pre-wedding shoot?
October to March – pleasant weather (15–28°C), clear skies, and that magical winter light. Avoid peak summer (April–May) due to heat.
❓ Can we shoot at Quarry Sunset Point without permission?
Inform local panchayat before any commercial shoot. Stay at least 10 feet from quarry edges, carry all trash, avoid weekends.
❓ How many outfits for a 4-hour shoot?
2-3 outfits ideal: start with formal (lehenga/suit), switch to casual (coordinated western wear), end with flowy for golden hour.
❓ What if it rains on the scheduled day?
We offer free rescheduling. Many couples use indoor backup options: Bangalore Palace interiors (with permit) or cosy Church Street cafes.
❓ What if a location rule changes after this guide?
Always verify with local authorities before visiting. This guide is informational – rules may change. Be responsible and obtain written permissions.
⚠️ Important Notice: The permissions, timings and rules above are based on publicly available information as of 2026. If any fact above is currently not permitted or may change, use this guide for informational purpose only. Always do your own research before visiting, contact local authorities, and be a responsible photographer. Respect public spaces, obtain written permissions, and leave no trace.
