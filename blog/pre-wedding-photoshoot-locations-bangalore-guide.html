📍 The Ultimate Top 10 Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations in Bangalore

1. 🌿 Cubbon Park – Where Nature and Old-World Charm Embrace

Right in the heart of the city, Cubbon Park feels like a different world. Towering rain trees, bamboo groves, colonial-era structures like the Bandstand and Attara Kacheri—this is where your love can breathe.

Go early in the morning, when the light filters soft through the canopy and the only sounds are birds and the gentle rustle of leaves. Walk hand-in-hand along shaded pathways. Let the photographer capture those quiet, in-between moments—the ones that feel lived-in, not staged.

  • Best time: 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM (golden light, fewer crowds)
  • Entry fee: Free
  • Pro tip: Incorporate the State Central Library building or the bamboo groves for unique textured backgrounds.
“Morning light filtering through the canopy creates soft, timeless portraits that feel calm and intimate.”

2. 🌸 Lalbagh Botanical Garden – A Love Story in Bloom

Over 240 acres of floral paradise, Lalbagh is a photographer’s dream. The iconic glasshouse (modeled after London’s Crystal Palace), serene lotus pond, rocky outcrops, and seasonal flower displays create endless variety.

Whether you’re aiming for dreamy, romantic portraits or playful candid shots, Lalbagh delivers. The golden hour here, just before sunset, is especially enchanting—the glasshouse glows, and your images take on a cinematic quality.

  • Best time: 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM or 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM (soft, flattering light)
  • Entry fee: Approx. ₹20–₹30 per person (minimal)
  • Pro tip: The Japanese decorative monument and the lake near the bandstand offer unexpectedly beautiful frames.

3. 👑 Bangalore Palace – Royal Elegance for Your Love Story

If you’ve ever dreamed of feeling like royalty—even for a day—Bangalore Palace is your answer. Tudor-style architecture, stained glass windows, sprawling grounds, and intricate woodwork create a backdrop of regal grandeur.

For the most intimate shots, stick to the exteriors and gardens (interiors require special permission). The palace looks especially majestic during golden hour, when warm light dances across its stone facades.

  • Best time: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM (soft light, fewer visitors)
  • Entry fee: Location charges may apply for professional shoots; check in advance
  • Pro tip: Visit on a weekday to avoid large crowds and secure those uninterrupted “fairytale” frames.

4. ⛰️ Nandi Hills – Cinematic Dreams Above the Clouds

Wake up before the world. Drive 60 km out of Bangalore, climb to 4,851 feet, and watch as mist and sunrise paint the sky in shades of gold and lavender. This is Nandi Hills.

It’s adventurous. It’s romantic. It’s absolutely cinematic. Whether you choose the hilltop for dramatic silhouette shots or the ancient Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple nearby for a touch of cultural depth, this location delivers frames that feel like movie stills.

  • Best time: 5:00 AM – 7:00 AM (sunrise; arrive early to beat weekend crowds)
  • Entry fee: Minimal (approx. ₹15–₹30 per person)
  • Pro tip: If you want mist-shrouded ethereal shots, plan your visit during the cooler months from October to March.

5. 💒 Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace – A Glimpse into Royal History

For couples who adore heritage and storytelling, Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace offers Indo-Islamic architecture at its finest. Intricate wooden pillars, arched balconies, and open courtyards make for regal, traditional-themed portraits.

  • Best time: 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM (good natural light)
  • Entry fee: Approx. ₹15 per person
  • Pro tip: Combine this with a visit to the nearby Bangalore Fort for varied historical backdrops.

6. 🌊 Hesaraghatta Lake – Where the Sky Meets the Earth

A hidden gem about 18 km from the city, Hesaraghatta Lake offers open grasslands, serene waters, and spectacular sunset views. During winter, you’ll also spot migratory birds, adding life and movement to your frames.

  • Best time: 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM (golden hour magic)
  • Entry fee: Free
  • Pro tip: Carry water and snacks—facilities are minimal, but the views are unmatched.

7. 🎨 Church Street – Urban Cool, Colourful and Vibrant

Does your love story feel young, breezy, and full of life? Church Street is for you. With its colonial façades, quirky cafés, bookshops, and vibrant graffiti walls, this is where your pre-wedding album gets an urban, candid edge.

  • Best time: 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM
  • Entry fee: Free
  • Pro tip: Pair your outfits with the graffiti for a fun, pop-art look.

8. 🌸 Ulsoor Lake – Reflections of Romance

In the heart of the city, Ulsoor Lake offers calm water, lush surroundings, and picturesque islands. Sunrise and sunset here are magical—the lake mirrors the sky, and the whole scene feels like poetry.

  • Best time: 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM (sunrise) or 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM (sunset)
  • Entry fee: Free (boating available at additional cost)
  • Pro tip: Visit during the week to avoid weekend crowds and ensure a peaceful session.

9. 🏛️ Vidhana Soudha – Majestic Architecture After Dark

For something bold and dramatic, Vidhana Soudha’s neo-Dravidian architecture is stunning. While interiors are restricted, the exterior—especially when floodlit on Sunday evenings—creates a majestic, awe-inspiring backdrop.

  • Best time: 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM (Sunday evenings for floodlights)
  • Entry fee: Free (exterior only)
  • Pro tip: There’s no photography fee since viewing is from public spaces, but be mindful of security and traffic.

10. 🌼 Sankey Tank – Peaceful Lakeside Bliss

Tucked away in Malleshwaram, Sankey Tank is a quiet reservoir with tree-lined pathways, a charming rose garden, and serene waters. It’s ideal for couples who want a relaxed, unhurried session away from the city’s hustle.

  • Best time: 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM
  • Entry fee: Free
  • Pro tip: The rose garden adds a vibrant pop of colour to your portraits—don’t miss it.

💚 The Greenery of Cubbon Park & Lalbagh

Cubbon Park – The Heart’s Quiet Beat

Cubbon Park isn’t just a park—it’s an experience. Spread over 300 acres, with ancient trees, flowering plants, bamboo groves, and colonial landmarks, it offers a serene, natural escape right in the city centre. Early mornings here are magical: the air is fresh, the light is golden, and the park feels like it belongs only to you.

Why it works for pre-wedding shoots: Diverse backdrops (bamboo groves, open lawns, historic buildings); soft diffused morning light; perfect for walk-and-shoot sessions.

Lalbagh – A Garden of Stories

Lalbagh isn’t just beautiful—it’s historic. Established in the 1700s, this botanical garden has been a sanctuary for nature lovers for centuries. The glasshouse, built in 1890, adds a touch of vintage elegance to your photos. Seasonal flower shows bring vibrant colours and fresh textures throughout the year.

Why it works for pre-wedding shoots: Iconic glasshouse and lotus pond; rocky outcrops; endless seasonal blooms.

“From the lush greenery of Lalbagh to the historical grandeur of Bangalore Palace, there’s a perfect spot for every kind of photoshoot.”

🏛️ Architectural Heritage Shoots

Bangalore is a treasure trove of architectural wonders. Whether you’re drawn to Indo-Islamic elegance, Tudor-style palaces, or neo-Dravidian landmarks, there’s a heritage backdrop waiting to frame your love.

Top architectural picks: Bangalore Palace, Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace, Vidhana Soudha, Attara Kacheri (within Cubbon Park).

Pro tip: For heritage shoots, early mornings or late afternoons provide the best natural light. Weekdays are quieter.

👗 Planning Your Outfits for Bangalore Weather

Bangalore’s weather is famously unpredictable—sunshine one moment, a gentle drizzle the next. The key to a stress-free shoot? Be prepared.

SeasonMonthsWeatherOutfit Tips
☀️ SummerMar–MayWarm (up to 34°C)Light fabrics (cotton, linen), pastel shades
☔ MonsoonJun–SepFrequent showersCarry umbrellas, quick-dry outfits, indoor backup
🍂 WinterOct–FebPleasant (15–28°C)Layers, light jackets, vibrant colours

Style Suggestions by Location: Cubbon Park/Lalbagh – flowing dresses, earthy tones; Bangalore Palace – formal wear, lehengas; Church Street – colourful streetwear; Hesaraghatta – maxi dresses, earthy neutrals.

Practical Checklist: ✅ Comfort first (walkable shoes) ✅ 2–3 outfit changes ✅ Umbrella, jacket, sunscreen ✅ Accessories (hats, jewellery).

💡 Why Professional Lighting Matters Outdoors

You’ve picked the perfect location. Your outfits are stunning. But if the lighting isn’t right, even the most beautiful setting can fall flat. Here’s the truth: light is the soul of a photograph.

The Golden Hour Magic

The hour just after sunrise and the hour just before sunset are called the golden hours. During these times, the sun is low, casting a soft, warm, diffused light that wraps everything in a gentle glow. Skin tones look flawless, shadows are soft, and the entire scene feels dreamy.

“Natural light is a wedding photographer’s best friend, especially for outdoor ceremonies and pre-wedding shoots. It offers a soft, flattering glow that enhances skin tones and creates timeless images.”

Why Professional Expertise Makes the Difference: Timing is everything; handling harsh sunlight with diffusers; overcast days as giant softboxes; creative lighting (backlighting, dappled shade).

Pro tip: Schedule your shoot during the dry months (October to March) for the most reliable outdoor conditions, but trust your photographer to adapt and create magic even in unexpected weather.
